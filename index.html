Colorado Water Restrictions

As of 08/26/2026 Denver has stated no spray irrigation after Sept. 30th 2026. Below is a link to Denver water restrictions.

Current watering guidelines and restrictions

How did Denver water make this decision?

Because of record low snow pack for the 2025-2026 snow season, record low rain fall throughout the summer, and customers failing to conserve enough water Denver has made the hard decision to end the current watering season early. This restriction is effect until Denver Water votes to end it. From early reports Denver water will not vote again until the spring of 2027.
